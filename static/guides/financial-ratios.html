Financial ratios and what they measure

A financial ratio relates two figures to answer a specific question. Check the formula, period, currency, and denominator before comparing it with another company.

Core ratio categories

Selected formulas used in StatementIQ
Category / measureFormulaQuestion
Liquidity · Current ratioCurrent assets ÷ current liabilitiesHow large is the short-term asset buffer?
Liquidity · Strict quick ratio(Cash + short-term investments + receivables) ÷ current liabilitiesWhat covers near-term liabilities without inventory?
Leverage · Debt-to-equityInterest-bearing debt ÷ shareholders’ equityHow much debt is carried relative to book equity?
Profitability · Operating marginOperating income ÷ revenue × 100%How much operating profit is retained per unit of sales?
Profitability · Net marginNet income ÷ revenue × 100%How much reported profit remains after expenses?
Efficiency · Asset turnoverRevenue ÷ average total assetsHow much revenue does the asset base generate?
Valuation · P/EShare price ÷ trailing earnings per shareWhat price is paid for reported earnings?

Why definitions matter

Debt-to-equity here uses interest-bearing debt, rather than all liabilities. The strict quick ratio includes specified liquid assets rather than simply subtracting inventory from current assets. Those definitions can differ from other websites.

Average-balance ratios need comparable beginning and ending balances. Missing balances or a denominator that is zero or economically unsuitable can make a result unavailable or not meaningful. Read each metric's displayed formula and source context.

Interpret ratios for the company

A bank's deposit liabilities do not behave like a manufacturer's trade financing. A software company may have little inventory. A utility can carry a different debt structure from a retailer. Use the business model and the company's history when interpreting the same ratio.

StatementIQ applies its documented company-type rules. It does not claim to have a complete specialist scoring model for every industry. The methodology page explains when a score is withheld and why some metrics provide context instead.

Health and valuation answer different questions

A financially resilient company can still have an expensive share price. A low multiple can reflect distress or falling earnings. The financial-health screen and valuation screen are kept separate so a cheap price does not automatically improve the assessment of financial condition.

Use ratios alongside the reported financial statements, cash flows, filing notes, and a forecast built from assumptions you can defend.
