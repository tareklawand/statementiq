About StatementIQ

StatementIQ is a financial statement analyzer for researching public companies. It brings reported company financials, financial ratios, financial-health screening, and discounted cash flow valuation into one workspace.

What you can research

Search a supported stock ticker to review its income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement. Compare reporting periods, inspect ratio formulas, review financial-health components, and examine a separate valuation screen.

The workspace also includes peer comparisons, annual-report review, public filing evidence, and a five-year forecast and DCF model where sufficient source data is available. Reports and model files can be downloaded without creating an account.

Sources and scope

StatementIQ uses Yahoo Finance for market data and standardized company financials, with SEC filing-recency checks and comparable Company Facts checks for supported SEC-reporting issuers. It can import supported SEC facts when provider statements are missing or behind. The source, reporting period, and verification status are shown with each analysis.

This website focuses on public-company fundamentals. It does not convert personal bank statements or transaction PDFs into spreadsheets. Coverage depends on the company, reporting system, available source fields, and provider access.

Using the results

Reported figures, calculated metrics, rules-based scores, and user-entered forecasts have different roles. A financial-health score is a research screen, not a credit rating or a recommendation to buy shares. Read the data and methodology page to understand missing inputs, sector limitations, and freshness checks.
